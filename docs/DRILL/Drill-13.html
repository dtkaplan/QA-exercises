NoteB. Do state variables influence one another?
  • Explain
  • 1.
  • 2.
  • 3.

In each of the following questions, a flow field is shown. In general, the rate of change of one component of the state can vary (or not) depending on itself or the other components of the state. (Here we have just two components.)

Exercise 8  

  1. True or False question or ? flow-tss-1-k3 The y-component of the flow depends strongly on the value of x.
Tip

In fact, the flow arrows don’t vary at all with x.

  1. True or False question or ? flow-tss-2-ws The x-component of the flow depends strongly on the value of y.
Tip

The x-component of flow is sometimes up and sometimes down. Which it is depends on the value of y.

  1. True or False question or ? flow-tss-3-8d The x-component of the flow depends strongly on the value of x.

  2. True or False question or ? flow-tss-4-is The y-component of the flow depends strongly on the value of y.

Exercise 9  

  1. True or False question or ? flow-gsl-1-k3 The y-component of the flow depends strongly on the value of x.
Tip

Focus, for instance, on the column of flow arrows stacked up at y = 0. For different values of x in that column, the flow arrows have different y components.

  1. True or False question or ? flow-gsl-2-ws The x-component of the flow depends strongly on the value of y.
Tip

Focus, for instance, on the row of flow arrows where x = 0. Moving left to right along the y axis, the x-component of the flow goes from zero to strongly negative.

  1. True or False question or ? flow-gsl-3-8d The x-component of the flow depends strongly on the value of x.

  2. True or False question or ? flow-gsl-4-is The y-component of the flow depends strongly on the value of y.
