Reading Question 15. 1 The marginal tax rate function (Figure 13.2a) and the total tax owed function (Figure 13.2b) are said to convey “the same information.” What is the relationship between them?

Reading Question 15. 2 The chapter describes the modeling logic behind the QALYs-versus-expenditure curves for Natal and Cancer (r xf(“fig-QALY-vs-cost”)`). What role does anti-differentiation play in that logic?

Reading Question 15. 3 In the US population example, the chapter gets a forecast of future population by “accumulating the rate of change” over future years. In plain language, what quantity is being accumulated and what is the with-respect-to quantity?

Reading Question 15. 4 The chapter says the main point is to know “whether the derivative or the anti-derivative might be a better form … for the purpose a model is being built for.” Give one example from the chapter where the marginal (derivative) form is more useful to work with, and one where the accumulated (anti-derivative) form is more useful.
